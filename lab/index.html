SCUBA: The Social Computing Laboratory

  • Ongoing projects
  • People

The Social Computing Laboratory (SCUBA) studies how digital environments shape human behavior and builds systems to make them healthier, safer, and more welcoming. SCUBA’s current research spans three threads:

  • Digital Governance Understanding what governance interventions actually work in online spaces.
  • Healthy Online Behavior Proactively cultivating desirable behavior through positive reinforcement.
  • Algorithmic & AI Audits Auditing how algorithms and AI systems shape attention, information exposure, and user wellbeing.

Our work broadly intersects with social computing, human-centered AI, natural language processing, and computational social science. SCUBA researchers have worked with large-scale platforms including Reddit, Facebook, Twitch, YouTube, and Discord, and our research has impacted their efforts to foster healthier online spaces.

SCUBA is a part of the Siebel School of Computing and Data Science at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign.

Ongoing projects

  • Models to Foster Healthy Conversations

    Measuring what drives positive feedback, surfacing community-valued behavior, and predicting prosocial conversation outcomes.

    Language of approval · Hidden values · Prosocial outcomes

  • Context-Aware AI for Online Governance

    Small and large language models for community-specific moderation, with transparent expert-style architectures for cross-community governance.

    MoMoE · SLM-Mod · LLM-Mod

  • Tools for Curating Social Feeds

    Benchmarks and models for value-aligned content curation (VASTU), expressive end-user controls for personalized feeds (Pilot on Bluesky), and moderation tooling that helps volunteers find and reward good contributions (Positive Queue).

    VASTU · Pilot · Positive Queue

  • Human-AI Systems for Online Safety

    Interactive visualization for threaded discussions, ML-guided panel review when moderators disagree, and AI-augmented interfaces for moderators on large-scale chat platforms.

    Needle · Venire · ConvEx

  • Causal Evidence for Behavior Change

    Randomized experiments isolating how feed rank steers attention, plus large-scale causal work on positive feedback from moderators and creators on Reddit and YouTube.

    Ranking effect · Positive reinforcement · Creator hearts

  • Audits of Algorithms and AI Systems

    Quasi-experimental studies of news and wellbeing on Bluesky, empirical audits of Reddit’s trending feed, and analyses of how algorithmic visibility shocks community activity.

    Hidden toll of news · Trending feed audit · Answer Bubbles

For a full list of papers, see publications. For code and artifacts, visit SCUBA on GitHub.
