I am a PhD student at Georgia Tech in Computer Science in the School of Interactive Computing. Previously, I obtained my B.Tech and M.Tech (Dual Degree) in Computer Science from Indian Institute of Technology, Madras. I am from the beautiful city of Chennai, India.
